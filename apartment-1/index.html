The neighbourhood

You live in the heart of Wedding, one of Berlin’s most lively and creative neighbourhoods, close to Mitte. Some of the city’s most exciting cultural spaces are a short walk away: concerts, film and festivals at Silent Green, art and talks at SAVVY Contemporary, exhibitions at Galerie Wedding, and nights out at Panke Culture by the river Panke. Dance studios and yoga, Pilates and CrossFit classes are within walking distance too.

Everyday life is easy too: groceries are 2 minutes from the front door, and two S-Bahn stations, the U6 and the U9 connect you to the rest of the city.
