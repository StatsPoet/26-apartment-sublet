The neighbourhood

You live in the heart of Wedding, one of Berlin’s most lively and creative neighbourhoods, close to Mitte. Some of the city’s most exciting cultural spaces are a short walk away: concerts, film and festivals at Silent Green, art and talks at SAVVY Contemporary, and exhibitions at Galerie Wedding.

Everyday life is easy too: groceries are 2 minutes from the front door, and the S-Bahn and U6 connect you to the rest of the city.
