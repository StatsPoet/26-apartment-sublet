The neighbourhood

Some of Berlin’s most exciting cultural spaces are a walk away: concerts, film and festivals at Silent Green, art and talks at SAVVY Contemporary, and exhibitions at Galerie Wedding. Schillerpark, the Schiller-Bibliothek, a cinema and the coffee shops of the Sprengelkiez are close by as well.

Everyday life is easy too: groceries are 2 minutes from the front door, the U6 and U9 are 5 minutes away, and trams and buses go straight to Prenzlauer Berg, Pankow and the central station. The neighbours in the house are friendly.
